The challenge

Loved offline since 1960. Invisible online.

Every sale happened at the brand's own counters. Shoppers looking online couldn't find it, let alone buy it.

  • Offline only

    A $1M+ brand whose sales all came through its physical stores.

  • No website

    No storefront of its own, so no way to order directly.

  • Not on marketplaces

    Missing from Amazon and Flipkart, where online snack shoppers search.

What I did

Three sales channels, built from zero.

Each one closes a gap from the list above.

  1. 01

    Website development

    Built the online storefront, so customers can browse the range and order directly.

    Why: the brand gets a store it owns, with its own customers.

    Solves: no website

  2. 02

    Amazon launch

    Set up the brand's Amazon sales channel and created its product listings.

    Why: Amazon is where a lot of online shoppers start their search.

    Solves: not on marketplaces

  3. 03

    Flipkart expansion

    Established Flipkart as a second marketplace channel.

    Why: more online shoppers reached, and sales don't hang on one platform.

    Solves: sales stuck offline
