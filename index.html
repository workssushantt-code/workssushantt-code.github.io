In the founder’s words, it was a “nice product, sketchy website” situation. And nobody types their card details into a store that feels sketchy.

Overview

A brand people love. Just not online.

Best Bites Namkeen has been selling sev, mixtures and namkeen since 1960. It’s a $1M+ business with its own stores, and every sale came through the counter.

Here’s the catch. When someone outside the city wants your snacks, they open Amazon. If you’re not there, they buy someone else’s. Every single day.

My role

  • Opened a new revenue stream beyond the physical stores
  • Built a strong online presence that brings in orders every month
  • Made Best Bites easy to find and buy, wherever shoppers live

where it started

The problem

Great snacks. Invisible online.

People loved the snacks. But they could only buy them at the shop. If you lived far away, you could not buy them at all.

The problem in one lineBest Bites didn’t have a snack problem. It had a “can’t buy it online” problem.

The goal

What winning looked like.

For the brand

  • Steady online orders every month, starting from zero.
  • Sales from more than one channel, so no single platform holds all the power.

For the shopper

  • Find Best Bites wherever they already shop online.
  • Order a favourite namkeen in a few taps.

How I fixed it

Three sales channels, built from zero.

  1. 01

    Turned an offline brand into an online sales channel

    0 → 127 monthly orders in 45 days

    I built Best Bites’ own direct-to-customer store and turned its online presence into a channel that brings in sales.

    • Lifted mobile site conversion to 2.4%
    • Grew organic traffic on the website from 0 to 1.8K in 60 days with SEO, AEO and GEO
    • Built a base for long-term organic traffic and new customers

    The result: 127 monthly orders and a new revenue channel beyond the physical stores.

  2. 02

    Turned Amazon into a consistent sales channel

    7.8x ROAS · 3K–4K monthly traffic

    I made Best Bites easy to find for shoppers already searching for snacks on Amazon.

    • Ran and optimized Amazon Ads at 7.8x ROAS
    • Grew Amazon traffic to about 3,000–4,000 visits a month
    • Built a branded Amazon storefront and optimized the product listings

    The result: A growing marketplace sales channel, with ad returns you can measure and more shoppers reached every month.

  3. 03

    Expanded Best Bites to a second marketplace

    Amazon + Flipkart · 2 marketplace channels

    I took Best Bites onto Flipkart, opening one more place for shoppers to find and buy it.

    • Launched the product range on Flipkart
    • Built product listings to improve marketplace visibility

    The result: Best Bites now sells beyond Amazon, reaching shoppers on two major marketplaces.
